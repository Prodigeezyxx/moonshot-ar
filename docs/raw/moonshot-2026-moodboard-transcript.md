## What this adds to the known spec

- Confirms **event branding**: "Moonshot 2026 · Courage & Conviction", by
  TechCabal x Grey (Headline Sponsor).
- Confirms **RealmSpace (Floats XR) as the named technology partner** — "powered
  by realmspace" appears twice.
- Names **6 core installations** cleanly (page 14) — Signal, Arena, Circuit,
  Touch Terminal, Digital Twin, Smart Entry — which map reasonably well onto
  what's already in the master spec (Touch Terminal ≈ Wayfinder kiosk/PWA;
  Smart Entry ≈ badge check-in feeding the User/Session data model).
- Adds a **7th feature not previously documented**: an "AI Totem" conversational
  concierge ("Ask Moonshot") that answers natural-language questions and can
  route/schedule — this is new scope, not in the master spec's API/component
  list.
- Adds **zone names not previously listed**: AI Development, Climate Solutions,
  Creative Economy, Enterprise, Govt & Policy (alongside the already-known
  Fintech, Startup Festival) — more granular than the 5-zone version in
  ARCHITECTURE.md §7. Also introduces "FUEL" as a deal-room/investor-meeting
  zone name distinct from the 9-track session system.
- Adds **gamified activations** beyond the quest/XP/badge system already
  spec'd: an arcade "Pitch Game" and a "Table Soccer — Courage & Conviction
  Cup" tournament with a live scoreboard — physical, not app-based, engagement.
- Surfaces the **beacon contradiction** flagged above.

These are folded into `ARCHITECTURE.md` with explicit confirm-needed flags
rather than silently treated as locked scope, since this is a rendered
moodboard (aspirational pitch visuals), not a written technical commitment.
